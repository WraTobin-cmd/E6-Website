05 / FAQ
What is E6?

E6 is a multiplayer VR game combining unique gameplay ideas from many different games into one evolving world. The game takes place on a planet with an underground society and locations across the surface and below it.

What backend does E6 use?

E6 uses RiftNet and E6 Core, backend systems designed to support the E6 ecosystem and connect its game services.

What is E6 made in?

Etherion 6 is made in Unity.

How can I become an EA Tester?

Join the Etherion 6 community and use the current EA Tester application process. Testing waves open when enough applications have been collected or when more testers are needed.

How often are testing waves?

EA Testing Waves happen when there is enough demand for applications or when the development team needs additional testers.

How can I join the Etherion Studios development team?

Contact the Etherion Studios team through the community's application process. Successful applicants may be invited to an interview.
